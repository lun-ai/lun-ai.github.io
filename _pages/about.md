I am postdoctoral research fellow at EMBL-EBI. I am intrigued about AI's possibilities in reshaping people's lives and our understanding of the world. 

`Interests`:
I aim to develop sustainable and user-friendly AI technologies that can help us tackle pressing scientific and societal challenges. I specialise in AI in Biology, eXplainable AI ([XAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Explainable_artificial_intelligence)) and logic-based AI ([Inductive Logic Programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inductive_logic_programming)).

`Networks`:
- EMBL's European Bioinformatics Institute [EMBL-EBI](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/) (2025 -)
- UKRI BBSRC [AI-4-EB](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/news/236657/new-uk-wide-ai-engineering-biology-consortium/)(2022 - 2025)
- EU Horizon [TAILOR](https://tailor-network.eu/) (2020 - 2023)
- UKRI EPSRC Network+ [Human-Like Computing](http://hlc.doc.ic.ac.uk/) (2019 - 2024)

`Doctoral research`: 
I built AI systems that taught people to play games and perform advanced algorithms (Ultra-Strong Machine Learning). I obtained my doctoral degree under the supervision of Prof. [Stephen Muggleton](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=WxJXT2MAAAAJ&hl=en), in collaboration with Prof. [Ute Schmid](https://www.uni-bamberg.de/en/cogsys/schmid/), a cognitive scientist from University of Bamberg.
